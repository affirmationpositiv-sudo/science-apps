## 2. The Solution: 3 Steps — 100 % Free

### Step 1: The "One Dollar a Day" Start (Earth — Body)

**The Problem:** You think retirement saving requires $500/month or a big lump sum.

**The Truth:** Start with $1/day. Seriously.

**How to do it for free:**

1. **Open a free brokerage account** — Robinhood, Fidelity, Schwab (all have $0 minimums)
2. **Set up an automatic transfer** — $1/day from checking to your investment account
3. **Buy into a free ETF** — VTI or VOO (0.03 % expense ratio, basically free)

> **Why $1/day instead of $30/month?** Psychology. $1/day doesn't feel like sacrifice. It's a **habit hack**. After 40 days it's routine. Then you bump it to $2.

**The IKUNE connection:** In the Earth stage (Days 1–40) you build **physical discipline** — one action, every day, no exceptions. That's exactly what your retirement needs.

> **Your Day 1 task:** Set up the automatic transfer. 5 minutes. Done.
> **Your Days 2–40:** Don't touch it. Just let it run. Check once a day (30 seconds).

### Step 2: The Solo 401(k) Tax Hack (Water — Emotions)

**The Problem:** You're afraid of tax forms. Or you procrastinate on them. That procrastination costs you real money.

**The Misconception:** "Retirement savings don't help me now."

**The Reality:** As a US freelancer, a **Solo 401(k)** lets you contribute **$23,000 employee-side + up to $46,000 employer-side** (2026 limits). And it's all **pre-tax** — meaning you pay $0 in income tax on every dollar you contribute.

**3-step free plan:**

1. **Open a Solo 401(k)** — Vanguard, Fidelity, Schwab — all free to open, $0 fees
2. **Contribute $50/month to start** — yes, that's enough
3. **Claim the deduction on your Schedule C** — TurboTax Free Edition handles this

> **The math:** $50/month × 30 years × 7 % market return = **$60,750**. Plus tax savings of ~$180/year. Minus $0 in fees. You don't need a financial advisor for this.

**The IKUNE connection:** In the Water stage (Days 41–80) you confront your **emotions** — especially the fear of financial decisions. Write down: "What exactly am I afraid will happen if I open a Solo 401(k)?" Then do it anyway.

> **Your Day 41 task:** Open the Solo 401(k). 15 minutes. Free.
> **Your Days 42–80:** Let the $50/month contribution run. Don't optimize. Don't change it. Just let it run.
